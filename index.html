Featured engineering · 2026

Bharat-Router

A cost-aware LLM router for Hinglish and code-mixed queries, built on RouteLLM (LMSYS, 2024). I built a 200-prompt parallel Hinglish/English benchmark across 7 domains, and measured how today's routers and small models behave when Indians type the way they talk.

Architecturev0.1

A language-aware router

Each query goes to the cheapest path that can answer it well — cache, a 3B model, translate-then-small, or a large model. A judge scores the answers and those scores retrain the router.
